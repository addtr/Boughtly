Connect Four

Privacy Policy

Last updated July 25, 2026

Connect Four is a simple offline game built to respect your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what happens to your data — and what doesn't.

The short version

Connect Four has no accounts and no server. We don't collect your personal information. The only data that leaves your device is the advertising data collected by our ad partner to show ads — and the Remove Ads purchase turns even that off. See "Advertising" below.

What we collect

We don't collect personal information about you. Connect Four has no login and no user profile. Your game preferences — such as the theme, sound and haptics toggles, who goes first, the series length, and whether you've removed ads — are saved only on your own device so the app remembers them. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

No camera, contacts, or location

Connect Four does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos, and we do not collect your location. It's just a board game.

Advertising

To keep the game free, we show ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier and limited technical data (such as device type, general location, and how you interact with ads). This does not include any game data — there isn't any personal game data to share.

On iOS we ask, through Apple's "Allow Tracking?" prompt, before any personalized advertising. If you decline, you'll still see ads, but non-personalized ones. You can change this anytime in your device Settings under Privacy → Tracking. Google's use of advertising data is governed by its own policies at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying Remove Ads turns off all ads, and no advertising data is collected after that.

Purchases

Connect Four offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your payment details. The app only records, on your device, whether the upgrade is active.

Analytics

We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs to profile you inside the game. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregate App Store statistics (such as total downloads), which do not identify you.

Children

Connect Four is suitable for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where required, ads are served in non-personalized mode.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date above. If the game ever adds features that send new data off your device, we'll update this policy before turning them on.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email addtr.labs@gmail.com.
